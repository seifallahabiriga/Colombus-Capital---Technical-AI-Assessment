# Tunisian Dinar (TND) Investment Analysis: Technical AI Assessment

## Project Overview
[cite_start]This repository contains the technical analysis and data visualization supporting a **Short Position** recommendation for the Tunisian Dinar (TND)[cite: 5, 14]. [cite_start]The analysis was conducted as part of the Colombus Capital Technical AI Assessment[cite: 1, 2].

## Thesis Summary
[cite_start]While short-term indicators such as easing inflation and stable FX reserves suggest a neutral outlook, the structural erosion of central bank independence and the monetization of the fiscal deficit present significant downside risks[cite: 23, 27]. This research adopts a risk-seeking "Short" conviction based on:
* [cite_start]**Institutional Decay:** Recent legislation and the 2025 Finance Law allowing direct, zero-interest loans from the Central Bank to the government[cite: 10].
* [cite_start]**Fiscal Pressure:** Public debt exceeding 80% of GDP and the absence of an IMF safety net[cite: 9, 11].
* [cite_start]**Long-term Trends:** A 40.6% depreciation against the USD over the last decade[cite: 10].
